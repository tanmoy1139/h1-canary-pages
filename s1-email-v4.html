Maple Leaf Tech Deals — Brampton weekly roundup

Published this week · 4 min read

Welcome back to the roundup. This week we tracked price drops on robot vacuums, mechanical keyboards, and standing desks across three GTA retailers. The vacuum shortlist is unchanged from last week: the mid-range bagless model still wins on noise, while the premium unit wins on mapping.

Keyboard corner

Two hot-swappable boards went on sale. If you type all day, prioritize the gasket-mounted one; the tray-mount is louder but cheaper. Keycap sets are personal — we won't pretend otherwise.

Standing desks

Single-motor frames are fine under 100 kg of gear. Above that, pay for the dual-motor. Cable trays are never included and always worth it.

That's the roundup. See you next week.
